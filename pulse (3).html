    Under the hood

    Reliable AI, not a lucky prompt.

    Four layers keep Pulse useful when the AI is brilliant, slow, or unavailable.

    1 · CAPTURE

    Any input

    Type, speak, upload notes or snap a photo of a to-do list. Deadlines also flow in from your calendar.

    2 · REASON

    AI plans

    Ranks by deadline, effort and energy, then returns strict JSON with a reason for every block.

    3 · VERIFY

    Code checks the AI

    Output is sanitised and the time budget is enforced in code, so a plan never exceeds the minutes you have.

    4 · PROTECT

    Safe fallback

    If AI is down, an offline planner still answers. Distress language pauses planning and points to real people.

    Responsible by design

    Helpful, honest, and human.

    AI that’s transparent

    Each plan shows whether it came from the AI or the offline fallback, so you always know.

    Wellbeing first

    If a message signals serious distress, Pulse pauses planning and points you to real people instead.

    Private by default

    Pulse has no server. Your calendar, stats and today’s plan are saved only in this browser. If you add a Gemini key, the text you submit is sent to Google to get AI replies.
